Boneset — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Boneset is a domino puzzle. It has no accounts of its own, no chat and no sign-up form, so it never learns your name, e-mail or anything you would call an identity. What it does collect is a random player id plus a record of how the puzzles are going — enough to tell a board that is too hard from a board that is broken.

Who to write to

Questions, access requests, deletion requests — one address: bonesetpuzzle@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your progress through the campaign and the log of boards you have solved.
  • Settings: language, sound and vibration.
  • An unfinished board, so you can come back to it.
  • A random player id (a UUID, generated on your device the first time you play). It is not derived from anything about you or your hardware.

In the browser this is ordinary site storage; in the app it is the app's own storage on your device. Clearing site data — or uninstalling the app — removes all of it, the player id included.

Inside Telegram the same set is kept in Telegram's cloud storage for this Mini App instead of on the device: it is tied to your Telegram account, so your progress follows you between devices, and Telegram holds it under its own terms. Telegram also hands every Mini App its launch data — your Telegram user id, name, username and interface language. The game reads the language only; it never stores the rest and never sends it anywhere.

What leaves your device

  • Gameplay analytics, to PostHog on EU servers: named in-game events — the app opening and the session ending, a level started, solved or abandoned, the first move, hints and auto-fill, mode switches, pauses, board sharing, language, sound and vibration changes, generator use, and errors. Each event carries the random player id, the game version and platform, your browser and device type, and the IP address of the request, from which PostHog derives an approximate country and city.
  • Leaderboard entries, to a Supabase database: when you solve a board, its completion time, the player id and the platform. The game can ask only for its own place in the table — it cannot read anyone else's rows.
  • The end-of-game survey, if you fill it in: your rating and whatever you type in the comment. It is a free-text field, so please don't put personal details in it.

What the game never does

  • No names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, contacts or payment details.
  • No advertising profiles, no cross-site tracking, no data brokers.
  • Your data is never sold or handed to anyone beyond the services listed below.

Why we collect it

Legitimate interest in making the game work: seeing where players get stuck, which boards go unsolved, and what crashes. The data is pseudonymous — it hangs on a random id, and we have no way to walk back from that id to a person.

How long it is kept

Analytics events are kept while they still say something about how the game plays, and are dropped when they stop being useful. A leaderboard row lives as long as that board's table does. Both go sooner if you ask.

Getting your data out or deleted

Clearing the site's data — or uninstalling the app — wipes everything held on your device, and the game starts over with a brand-new id.

For the records already sent, write to bonesetpuzzle@gmail.com. Everything is keyed by the random player id, so include it, or the records cannot be found. Open this page from the game — Menu → Privacy & data — and your id appears right here:

Who processes the data

  • PostHog — product analytics, EU region.
  • Supabase — the leaderboard database.
  • Telegram — cloud storage of the Mini App, only when you play inside Telegram.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Nothing here is retroactive: data already collected keeps the terms it was collected under.
